# Emergency Download (EDL) mode
{:.no_toc}

Dangerous
{:.label.label-red.mx-0.mt-0}

{:.caution}
> Overwriting system partitions on your device can render it unbootable. Be sure to [back up your data] before proceeding.

<ins>E</ins>mergency <ins>D</ins>own<ins>l</ins>oad mode, commonly known as EDL mode, is a special boot mode implemented on devices with Qualcomm chipsets. It allows low-level access to the device's partition table, and is used for reading and writing system partitions, recovering devices, or performing other operations.
